## Affidavit 1: Format for Establishment of New Institution / Extension of Approval
*(To be submitted on Non-Judicial Stamp Paper of minimum Rs. 100/- duly notarized)*

I/We, `<Name of Deponent>`, Son/Daughter of `<Parent Name>`, `<Age>`, resident of `<Address>`, in my capacity as `<Chairman/Secretary>` of `<Name of the Trust/Society/Company>`, do hereby solemnly affirm, state, and declare as under:

1. That the `<Name of the Trust/Society>` has applied for `<Grant of New Institute / EoA>` for `<Name of the Institution>` situated at `<Address>` for the Academic Year 2026-27.
2. That all particulars, documents, certificates, land deeds, and statements uploaded on the AICTE web portal are genuine, true, and correct to the best of my knowledge and belief.
3. That the land on which the institution is situated is in legal possession of the Trust/Society and is unencumbered by any third-party mortgage, lien, or litigation, except for loans taken from authorized nationalized banks for educational infrastructure development.
4. That the institution strictly adheres to the AICTE norms regarding Student-Faculty Ratio (SFR), Cadre Ratio, and payment of scales as per the 7th Central Pay Commission (CPC).
5. That if any document or statement submitted is found to be false, forged, altered, or fabricated, AICTE shall be free to withdraw approval, lodge criminal prosecution under relevant sections of the Indian Penal Code / Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, and initiate forfeiture of security deposits without notice.

**DEPONENT**  
Verified at `<Place>` on this `<Day>` of `<Month>`, 2026.

## Affidavit 2: Anti-Ragging & Non-Commercialization Undertaking
Affidavit declaring:
- Strict zero-tolerance implementation of AICTE (Prevention and Prohibition of Ragging in Technical Institutions) Regulations.
- Constitution of Anti-Ragging Committee and Internal Complaints Committee (ICC).
- Non-collection of capitation fees or unapproved commercial charges.
