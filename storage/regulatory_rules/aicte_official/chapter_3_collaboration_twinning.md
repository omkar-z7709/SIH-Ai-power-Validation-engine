# AICTE Approval Process Handbook (APH) - Chapter 3: Collaboration & Joint Degrees (Twinning & Dual Degree)
**Source:** All India Council for Technical Education  
**Regulatory Cycle:** 2024-2027 (Extended through 2026-27)  
**Document Ref:** AICTE/APH/CH-03/COLLAB  

---

## 3.1 Scope of Collaboration & Twinning Programs
3.1.1 Foreign Universities and Indian Technical Institutions collaborating for Twinning or Dual Degree programs must obtain prior approval of AICTE.

3.1.2 Only Indian Institutions with NBA accredited programs (minimum 2 cycles or valid accreditation) or NAAC grade 'A+' or above shall be eligible for collaboration with foreign educational entities ranked in top 500 QS/THE rankings.

---

## 3.2 Required Document Package
1. **MoU Agreement:** Duly executed bilateral Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) defining credit transfer, curriculum parity, and degree awarding clauses.
2. **AIU Equivalence:** Association of Indian Universities (AIU) equivalence certificate for foreign degree parity.
3. **Foreign Ministry / Embassy Clearance:** No Objection Certificate (NOC) from the relevant diplomatic mission / Ministry of External Affairs.
4. **Governing Board Resolutions:** Formal resolutions approving collaborative quota from both participating institutions.
