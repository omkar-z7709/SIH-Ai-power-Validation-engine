# AICTE Approval Process Handbook (APH) - Chapter 4: Increase in Intake, Additional Courses & Structural Variations
**Source:** All India Council for Technical Education  
**Regulatory Cycle:** 2024-2027 (Extended through 2026-27)  
**Document Ref:** AICTE/APH/CH-04/INTAKE-VARIATION  

---

## 4.1 Conditions for Expansion & Variation in Intake
4.1.1 An existing Technical Institution shall be eligible for increase in intake / additional course(s) subject to:
- Valid accreditation from the National Board of Accreditation (NBA) for at least 50% of the eligible programs.
- Minimum 3 consecutive years of existence with approved Extension of Approval.
- Clean regulatory track record without pending disciplinary penalty or admission freeze.

---

## 4.2 Document Scrutiny Checklist
1. **Valid NBA / NAAC Accreditation Order:**
   - Order must cover the department where intake increase or new program is sought.
2. **University Approval / NOC:**
   - Formal university NOC approving syllabus, examination capacity, and affiliation for the augmented intake.
3. **Built-Up Area & Lab Space Audit:**
   - Architect certificate confirming built-up area augmentation meeting Appendix 4 norms (Instructional, Administrative, and Amenities area).
4. **Faculty Roster Plan:**
   - Detailed recruitment schedule to preserve prescribed Cadre Ratio (1 Professor : 2 Associate Professors : 6 Assistant Professors).
