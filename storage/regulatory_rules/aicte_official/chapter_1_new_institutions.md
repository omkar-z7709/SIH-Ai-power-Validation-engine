# AICTE Approval Process Handbook (APH) - Chapter 1: Grant of Approval for New Institutions
**Source:** All India Council for Technical Education (Statutory Body under Ministry of Education, Govt. of India)  
**Regulatory Cycle:** 2024-2027 (Extended through 2026-27)  
**Document Ref:** AICTE/APH/CH-01/NEW-INST  

---

## 1.1 Introduction and Objective
1.1.1 A Society/Trust/Company established under Section 8 of Companies Act, 2013 intending to establish a new Technical Institution offering Degree/Diploma programs in Engineering & Technology, Management, Pharmacy, Architecture, Planning, Applied Arts & Crafts, and Hotel Management & Catering Technology shall apply online on the AICTE Web-Portal.

1.1.2 Scrutiny of the application and submitted credentials shall be carried out by a Scrutiny Committee constituted by the Regional Officer as per the norms laid down in this Handbook.

---

## 1.2 Mandatory Supporting Documents (Initial Scrutiny Checklist)
Every applicant institution MUST upload legible copies of the following statutory documents:
1. **Registered Land Documents:**
   - Registered Sale Deed / Gift Deed / Government Lease Agreement (minimum 30 years).
   - Non-Agricultural (NA) Land Conversion Certificate issued by the District Revenue Authority / Competent Authority.
   - Land Use Certificate specifying educational institutional purpose.
2. **Approved Building Plan & Occupancy:**
   - Building Plan approved by the Competent Town Planning / Municipal Authority.
   - Building Completion Certificate / Occupancy Certificate issued by the Local Municipal Authority.
   - Structural Stability Certificate signed by a registered Chartered Structural Engineer.
   - Fire and Life Safety Certificate (NOC) from the State Fire Service Department.
3. **University / State Board Affiliation:**
   - Letter of Intent (LoI) / NOC / Provisional Affiliation Order from the affiliating University or State Technical Education Board.
4. **Statutory Affidavit & Undertaking:**
   - Duly notarized Affidavit on non-judicial stamp paper of prescribed value (Affidavit 1 & 2) signed by the Chairman/Secretary of the Trust/Society.
5. **Entity Registration:**
   - Registration Certificate of the Trust/Society/Company along with Memorandum of Association (MoA) and Bylaws.

---

## 1.3 Strict Legal Verification Rules
- **Rule 1.3.1 (Legal Identity Consistency):** The exact legal name of the Trust/Society in the Registration Certificate must match verbatim across the Land Deed, Bank Accounts, Affiliation Order, and AICTE Application Form.
- **Rule 1.3.2 (Land Contiguity):** Land must be in a single continuous piece or maximum two contiguous plots separated by a public road with an approved connecting overbridge or subway.
- **Rule 1.3.3 (Signatory Authority):** All affidavits, undertakings, and forms must be signed by the authorized deponent designated by resolution of the Governing Board.
