# AICTE Approval Process Handbook (APH) - Chapter 2: Grant of Extension of Approval (EoA)
**Source:** All India Council for Technical Education  
**Regulatory Cycle:** 2024-2027 (Extended through 2026-27)  
**Document Ref:** AICTE/APH/CH-02/EOA  

---

## 2.1 Scope of Extension of Approval
2.1.1 Existing Technical Institutions intending to continue offering approved programs, change intake, add new courses, or seek continuation of approval must submit an online application for Extension of Approval (EoA) for the relevant academic year.

2.1.2 Approval shall be granted based on self-disclosure, statutory regulatory reports, compliance inspections, and automated document scrutiny.

---

## 2.2 Mandatory Documents for EoA
1. **Continuation of University Affiliation:**
   - Formal grant of Continuation of Affiliation order from the affiliating University valid for the specific Academic Year.
2. **Accreditation Status Certificate:**
   - Valid Accreditation Certificate from the National Board of Accreditation (NBA) or National Assessment and Accreditation Council (NAAC), where applicable.
3. **Faculty & Infrastructure Compliance Affidavit:**
   - Affidavit affirming compliance with Student-Faculty Ratio (SFR: 1:15 for Degree Engineering, 1:20 for Management/Diploma), Cadre Ratio, and payment of 7th CPC salary scales.
4. **Fire Safety & Building Safety Validity:**
   - Current valid Fire Safety Certificate from the Fire & Emergency Services Department.
   - Water and Environmental Clearance Certificates.

---

## 2.3 Regulatory Consistency Rules
- **Rule 2.3.1 (Date Inversion Prohibition):** Issue date of an affiliation or accreditation certificate can under no circumstances post-date its stated expiry date.
- **Rule 2.3.2 (Cycle Validity Window):** The certificate validity period must encompass the commencement date of the target academic cycle. Expired credentials without proof of active statutory renewal application trigger immediate hold.
- **Rule 2.3.3 (Intake Discrepancy Guard):** Approved intake stated in the institutional application cannot exceed the ceiling granted in the University Affiliation Order.
