# AICTE Approval Process Handbook (APH) - Appendices 4 & 5: Norms for Land, Built-Up Space, Computers & Faculty
**Source:** All India Council for Technical Education  
**Regulatory Cycle:** 2024-2027 (Extended through 2026-27)  
**Document Ref:** AICTE/APH/APP-04-05/NORMS  

---

## Appendix 4: Land & Building Space Norms
### Land Requirements by Location Type:
- **Mega Cities (Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, Chennai, Bangalore, Hyderabad):** Minimum 1.5 Acres for Engineering Degree.
- **Urban Areas (State Capitals, Municipal Corporations):** Minimum 2.5 Acres for Engineering Degree.
- **Rural Areas:** Minimum 7.5 Acres for Engineering Degree.

### Built-Up Area Standards:
- Instructional Area: Classrooms (66 sq.m each), Tutorial Rooms (33 sq.m each), Laboratories (66 sq.m each), Computer Center (150 sq.m), Library & Reading Hall (400 sq.m).
- Administrative Area: Principal's Office (30 sq.m), Board Room (20 sq.m), Faculty Cabins (5 sq.m per faculty), Central Store (30 sq.m).
- Amenities: Canteen, Restrooms, First-Aid Room, Boys/Girls Common Rooms (100 sq.m each).

## Appendix 5: Essential Equipment & Digital Norms
1. **Computer-to-Student Ratio:**
   - 1 computer per 4 students for Engineering programs.
   - 1 computer per 2 students for Management / MCA programs.
2. **Bandwidth:**
   - Minimum 100 Mbps dedicated optical fiber line for intake up to 300; 300 Mbps for intake up to 600.
3. **Library Resources:**
   - Minimum 100 titles and 500 volumes per course.
   - Mandatory subscription to National Digital Library (NDLI) and AICTE consortium e-journals.
4. **Safety & Inclusion:**
   - Barrier-free environment for Persons with Disabilities (PwD): Ramps, braille signs, and dedicated accessible washrooms.
   - All-weather CCTV surveillance at main entrance and examination halls.
